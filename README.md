# House of the Blackheads — a guided 360° walk

A demonstration tour of the House of the Blackheads in Tallinn: ten rooms of a
merchant brotherhood's house, from the facade of 1597 to the courtyard beneath the
White Hall, with the halls both empty and in use.

**Open the tour:** https://josebsreis.github.io/house-of-the-blackheads/

A demonstration of the work, not an official guide to the building.
